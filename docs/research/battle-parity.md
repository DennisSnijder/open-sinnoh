# Platinum battle mechanics parity

Audited 2026-10-16 against OpenSinnoh `60b31aba587c6d3092390606a3d9b78ffbf581e4`, pinned `@pkmn/sim` **0.10.11**, and pret/pokeplatinum **`fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c`**. This compares the **current adapter**, not just vanilla Showdown. Decomp sources were consulted for mechanics and names; no code or ROM-derived fixtures are included.

Adapter follow-up (2026-10-16, contracts 0.27.20): field weather/fog setup, fog suppression/Defog/Weather Ball/Solar Beam/recovery, E1, E2 and E5 now have synthetic unit coverage. E3 captures native HP at faint: earlier awards survive later-turn damage, and Self-Destruct users get no EXP even without an HP log line; full mid-turn progression still needs a sim pause/resume boundary. E4 has a controller pool-split regression. The audit tables below retain their baseline findings and separate sim-owned work.

Mechanics follow-up (2026-10-18, starting at `080b4d0`, same sim/decomp versions): the five previously unaudited areas are covered below. Local dex callbacks and a narrow accuracy action override carry the fixes; no simulator fork, importer output or shared contract changed.

The earlier [battle research](battles.md#14-how-exact-is-it-against-the-cartridge) assertion that damage rounding matches is too broad. The simple goldens in `packages/battle/src/damage.test.ts` establish only their tested cases. The table below separates remaining differences, already-adapted differences, and parity controls. It is a targeted audit of the requested systems, not exhaustive certification of every move/ability/item interaction.

**Fix boundary:** **Adapter** means our dex callbacks, PvE controller or data handoff can fix it. **Sim patch** means the move-resolution/event pipeline must change (a local mod overriding that pipeline still counts as a sim patch; an upstream fork is not necessarily required). **Model + adapter** needs persisted/input fields first. “Open” is source-confirmed unless marked a candidate; numerical probe results are identified separately from proposed replay expectations.

**Case convention:** all damage is integer HP, not percent. Unless a row overrides it: level 50, all relevant attacking/defending stats explicitly 100, neutral stages, no critical hit, no status, no weather, no item, and no damage-modifying ability. Named species supply types; use synthetic stat overrides for these mechanic-unit fixtures, or solve IVs/EVs/levels before writing a legal-party replay. Damage ranges enumerate rolls 85–100 inclusive and condition on hitting; listed 16-value vectors run from 100% down to 85% (r = 0…15). Status moves have damage **0–0** and an explicit state/order oracle. Do not compare cartridge and sim seeds as though their RNG call order were identical. Build original fixtures; any imported-species replay must use `romSlice()` and remain ROM-gated.

## Damage, criticals, abilities, items and action order

For ordinary damage, let `q = floor(floor((floor(2L/5)+2) × power × attack / defense) / 50)`, after the relevant power/stat adjustments. Platinum applies burn/screens, spread, weather and Flash Fire to that pre-`+2` quantity, truncating each integer operation; adds 2; applies the critical multiplier, Life Orb/Metronome/Me First; takes `floor(damage × (100 − rand%16)/100)` (minimum 1 at this step); then STAB and the type-chart entries. [Base stages](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c/src/battle/battle_lib.c#L6943-L7094), [post-base modifiers](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c/src/battle/battle_script.c#L1323-L1365). Fixed-damage moves and later survival/damage hooks are separate. The rows below show why matching the broad stage list is insufficient: truncation, modifier aggregation and the timing of Sniper still differ.

### Remaining boundaries

- **Contract/model request: remaining sleep across battles.** Core `Pokemon.status` stores an enum, not Platinum's remaining sleep bits. `controller.ts` `pushSim` initializes existing sleep to3 and the result cannot carry the counter back. Accurate battle-to-field-to-battle/save behavior needs a persisted sleep counter in the shared Pokémon model, with save validation/migration. This follow-up keeps the requested fixes in adapter/sim callbacks and does not change that shared model. The within-battle switch control must not be described as solving this.
- **Accepted RNG-stream difference remains:** modulo/bit-based cartridge draws versus the sim's scaled draws, including status rolls and multi-hit count selection. The marginal multi-hit distribution matches; same seeds are not a cartridge oracle.
- **Pursuit-triggered acid rain remains unimplemented/unprobed.** This is the decomp's documented field-mask corruption on a Pursuit KO during U-turn, not ordinary weather behavior. No claim of reproducing that glitch, every simultaneous-Pursuit KO chain, or every Transform/Mimic/Encore/Baton Pass/item interaction. The earlier damage rounding, spread sequencing and equal-Speed residual findings above remain open.
- These are headless mechanic regressions. The existing battle browser specs exercise integration and presentation; they do not independently measure cartridge outcomes or stage every synthetic edge case above.

Start with isolated mechanic fixtures that assert the complete 16-value damage vector or exact event sequence; then lift each into controller and ROM-gated replay coverage. Several proposed setups require stat, volatile, weather or RNG injection that the current text replay grammar does not expose. Add test-harness setup explicitly; do not silently approximate these fixtures with natural stats. For timing fixes, verify both the message sequence and the state used by subsequent attacks. Do not label an unprobed interaction a confirmed divergence. Ordinary Speed ties have the intended random winner distribution; the accepted stream mismatch is separate from the confirmed equal-Speed residual-order bug.
