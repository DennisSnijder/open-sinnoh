## How to read this map

- CP numbers preserve the original plan's replay boundaries; the proposed labels below correct and expand
  the original checkpoint list. Optional branches are identified explicitly.
- Map-local entry numbers are **one-based positions in the `ScriptEntry` table**. They are not the
  hexadecimal suffixes of old labels, NARC member IDs or byte offsets. Shared scripts have their own
  global IDs. The loader subtracts 1 for local IDs and a range base for shared IDs.
  [`src/script_manager.c:228–254`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/script_manager.c#L228-L254).
- Numeric trainer IDs are zero-based indexes in `generated/trainers.txt`, whose first entry is
  `TRAINER_NONE`. Suffixes on rival trainer IDs are keys selected from the **player's** original
  starter; do not infer Barry's party from the suffix alone.
  [`generated/trainers.txt:1–6`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/generated/trainers.txt#L1-L6),
  [`scripts_pokemon_league_north_pokecenter_1f.s:140–155`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/scripts/scripts_pokemon_league_north_pokecenter_1f.s#L140-L155).
- For ordinary event trainers, global script ID is **2999 + trainer ID**, or
  **4999 + trainer ID** when the event's double-battle side is 2. Thus the numeric IDs in
  the rosters also determine their shared script IDs; local entry numbers apply only to
  the map scripts explicitly identified below.
  [tools/jsoncnv/convert.py:79–83](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/tools/jsoncnv/convert.py#L79-L83).
- `FLAG_HIDE_* = 1` generally means the corresponding object is absent. Check the cited event's
  `hidden_flag` as well as script writes; trainer-defeat flags and object-hide flags are different.
  For example, the lighthouse removes Volkner and sets his returned-to-Gym flag; the event record
  separately names the object-hide flag.
  [`scripts_vista_lighthouse.s:62–74`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/scripts/scripts_vista_lighthouse.s#L62-L74),
  [`events_vista_lighthouse.json:44–57`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/events/events_vista_lighthouse.json#L44-L57).
- **Guaranteed** means an explicit progression script requires that battle/event. Ordinary trainer
  objects are inventoried where relevant, but a minimum unavoidable sight-trainer route requires
  collision/path analysis. Such uncertainty is marked, rather than presenting a walkthrough guess
  as a proven gate. A proposed replay can deliberately fight every listed trainer on its route.
- End assertions are **test proposals**, derived from the cited writes. They are not additional
  cartridge requirements. Keep a battle log as well as flags: Gym leaders often bulk-set their
  subtrainers' defeat flags, so those flags cannot prove the subtrainers were actually fought.
  [`scripts_sunyshore_city_gym_room_3.s:41–51`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/scripts/scripts_sunyshore_city_gym_room_3.s#L41-L51).
- “Beyond walk/talk” lists checkpoint-specific systems and `ScrCmd_*` handlers, not every ordinary
  text, branch, movement, sound or variable opcode. Asynchronous battle, animation and application
  completion must be respected. An unknown numeric command name remains unknown here.

## Limits and replay follow-up

The guaranteed script/event sequence above is source-traced. Minimum unavoidable ordinary
trainer sets, exact movement inputs, collision-safe Gym solutions and minimum field-move
loadouts remain **uncertain where marked**; no ROM replay was performed to resolve them.
The ordinary-trainer tables preserve concrete IDs and event locations for that follow-up.
The distinction matters: an object record proves presence, not that every path must cross its sight.

A future replay should assert both the checkpoint state and the actual battle log, serialize
and reload during persistent puzzles/partners, and cover losses, refused prompts and full-party
Egg handling separately. The proposed end states are designed as those tests' starting contract.
