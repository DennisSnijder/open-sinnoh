# Distortion World: source research and engine proposal

Research date: 2026-10-15. Source pin: pret/pokeplatinum `1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20`. This is a reading report, not a port: names, structure and behavior are described in original prose; no implementation, dialogue, game assets, ROM data, saves or screenshots are reproduced. Links below remain pinned so their line ranges are reviewable. “Proposal” means an engine design recommendation; “uncertain” means the cited source does not settle the detail.

## Script and movement surface

The command table explicitly registers `ScrCmd_InitPersistedMapFeaturesForDistortionWorld`, `ScrCmd_AddDistortionWorldMapObject`, `ScrCmd_DeleteDistortionWorldMapObject`, `ScrCmd_GetPlayer3DPos`, `ScrCmd_StartGiratinaOriginBattle`, `ScrCmd_ResetDistortionWorldPersistedCameraAngles`, `ScrCmd_DoDWWarp`, `ScrCmd_StartDistortionWorldGiratinaShadowEvent`, `ScrCmd_FinishDistortionWorldGiratinaShadowEvent`, and `ScrCmd_SetPartyGiratinaForm`. These are the Distortion World-specific integration set; ordinary locking, message, battle, object and camera commands remain necessary too. [registrations](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/include/data/scripts/scrcmd.h#L760-L816)

- Initialization delegates to persisted-map features; `GetPlayer3DPos` returns map-object X, **Y divided by two**, and Z into three script variables. Preserve this script-coordinate convention instead of returning raw render-space height. [handlers](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/scrcmd.c#L4780-L4800)
- Add/delete take a local-ID argument and delegate to the Distortion World object system; camera reset delegates to its persisted-camera handler. The special object enums start at local ID 128, with separate per-room names including lake guardians, boulders, pit text objects, Cynthia, Cyrus, Giratina, portals and the Griseous Orb. [handlers](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/scrcmd.c#L6995-L7018), [object identifiers](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/include/constants/distortion_world.h#L22-L93)

### Binary map-system format

The formats below describe the reader's schema, not copied archive content. Integer widths refer to DS C types (`int`/`u32`/`s32` = 32-bit; `u16`/`s16` = 16-bit). The structures and cursor arithmetic support the following little-endian importer design. The ABI-derived record sizes are map-info 12, platform 20, jump-point 40, camera 24, ghost header 12, ghost template 12 and ghost trigger 20 bytes; validate these with synthetic-parser and optional ROM checks rather than serializing host-language structs. The `*templates` members in the reader's convenience structs are not on-disk pointers: records are found immediately after the count. [src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c:575–752](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c#L575-L752) [src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c:4040–4091](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c#L4040-L4091) [src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c:4145–4249](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay009/ov9_02249960.c#L4145-L4249)

## Gravity, movement and collision

“Per-room gravity” is too coarse: **orientation is per floating platform**, with floor, west wall, east wall and ceiling states. Input directions are mapped to XYZ steps by four explicit tables. On either wall, up/down change Y; east/west walls reverse the corresponding Z direction. On the ceiling, up/down reverse Z while left/right retain X. The player dispatcher chooses orientation-specific walk/run/surf/collision actions. ## Design proposal for OpenSinnoh (proposed, not implemented)

### Existing seams and the smallest useful extension

At engine baseline `328cee14f3561888e32c1065ec03eed3f84ca0a4`, `MapGrid` indexes X/Z and solves Y from terrain, while avatar collision compares the next terrain height against the current height. The Distortion double jump currently logs a stub and bumps. These are the concrete places to extend movement, rather than teaching the renderer to change collision. ([engine tiles.ts:37–103](../../packages/world/src/tiles.ts), [avatar.ts:220–251](../../packages/world/src/avatar.ts), [avatar.ts:485–490](../../packages/world/src/avatar.ts))

`Camera` already has angles/projection/clips, but `FieldView.camera` publishes only a target and the separate offset; the renderer places the preset about that target. `ActorView` has no support orientation. DW commands are classified as M5, and movement decoding only handles the generic subset (including action 107 as a southward walk). ([engine records.ts:401–412](../../packages/contracts/src/records.ts), [runtime.ts:33–58](../../packages/contracts/src/runtime.ts), [runtime.ts:83–118](../../packages/contracts/src/runtime.ts), [renderer.ts:1235–1287](../../packages/render/src/renderer.ts), [classify.ts:29–38](../../packages/vm/src/classify.ts), [movement.ts:67–91](../../packages/world/src/movement.ts))

**Proposed ownership:** importer → engine-neutral records; world → support selection, movement, puzzle state, floor loading and camera timelines; VM → native command semantics and waits through `FieldHost`; renderer → immutable `FieldView` presentation. World, render and VM exchange contracts/core types only. Use discrete floor/west-wall/east-wall/ceiling support with authored transitions; arbitrary rigid-body gravity is unnecessary for this milestone.

### Step-by-step implementation plan

1. **Prove the formats.** Build original binary fixtures for each archive section, support orientation, jump/camera row and ghost group. Validate section lengths, counts, indices, bounds and enum/handler mappings at import. Add a dependency report per room and isolate any still-opaque field as explicitly pending. Do not infer a room's complete route from ordinary warp events.
2. **Ratify contracts and saves.** Add only the records/views/services above that the first proof needs, bump the contract version, and write migration/validation rules. Decide which resting native state must persist, including the distinction between a map change, a floor transition, battle return and a save reload.
3. **Prove orientation-aware collision in an original room.** Implement support-local coordinates and the four exact input direction bases. Resolve a candidate step in 3D, then query that support's attribute grid and 3D object occupancy. Avoid calling the global X/Z height solver on vertical supports. Check directional ledges, water and double jumps in their proper state. A nearby disconnected surface must not become reachable because its projected X/Z matches.
4. **Add authored transitions and floor loading.** Implement jump axes/arcs, direction changes, sprite rotations and exact task durations, including the split between camera and player transitions. Carry the player and relevant objects with moving platforms. Load active and required inactive floors at their offsets; a transition owns input/event gating and ends at an exact logical endpoint. Keep camera/chunk culling aware of rotated views and multiple visible floors.
5. **Run camera and renderer proofs together.** Publish resolved camera position/target/roll (or equivalent quaternion) and projection per tick; preserve native signed-angle/wrap rules rather than assuming shortest-path interpolation. Exercise billboard depth planes, rotated sprites, mount orientation, shadows/reflections and lighting during a camera roll. Draw sky BG/cloud layers beneath the field with the evidenced blend and tint behavior; keep cutscene framing usable on desktop and phone. Add material colour/alpha animation support where the converter audit identifies a gap.
6. **Implement special room events.** Add ghost groups, elevators, guardian/boulder events, fall targets/reset behavior, surf and both cascade directions. Apply collision-affecting changes on simulation ticks and visual clip phases through the view. Do not assume every disappearing prop is a collider. Treat native DW event commands separately from field-script bytecode while allowing both to start/wait for the same world task.
7. **Wire script semantics and story.** Implement the listed `ScrCmd_*` operations, opcode operands/results and yielding behavior; complete the DW movement actions. Exercise Cyrus victory/loss, Giratina capture/defeat/flee/loss, re-entry, portal choice and post-game Orb state without dialogue snapshots. Require zero encountered DW stubs before marking the route complete.
8. **Close the replay gate.** Only after the room proofs pass, enable the complete Spear Pillar → Sendoff Spring chain and the independent Turnback Cave branch. Remove sweep exemptions only when loading, traversal, state assertions and visual probes all pass.
