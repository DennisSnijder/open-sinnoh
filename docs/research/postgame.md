Research date: 2026-10-16. Primary reference: [pret/pokeplatinum at `fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/tree/fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c), inspected locally. These are original descriptions of names, gates and system responsibilities, with no copied implementation, dialogue, game-data tables, ROM material or screenshots.

The original plan named CP20 as National Dex → Battle Zone → Stark Mountain. The later [2026-10-14 scope decision](../decisions.md) includes the Battle Frontier in “complete”, at lower priority than main-story and post-game essentials. The facility detail here sizes the work and does not reopen that decision. This note proposes M6a/b/c; it does not claim any checkpoint or system already passes.

## Reading gates correctly

`FLAG_HIDE_*` controls object visibility; it is not necessarily a capture or completion flag. `VAR_MAP_LOCAL_*` and `VAR_0x800*` are script temporaries, not substitutes for persistent progress. A map script can establish availability while its battle handler records capture separately. The Hall of Fame also resets defeated, uncaptured encounters, so checking only the immediate battle result misses retry behavior. See its reset dispatch and individual handlers. [`res/field/scripts/scripts_pokemon_league_hall_of_fame.s:123–200`](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c/res/field/scripts/scripts_pokemon_league_hall_of_fame.s#L123-L200)

The opcode audit is against this repository's `packages/vm/src/data/op-classification.json` at research time: 840 commands, 345 classified implemented, 422 stubbed and 73 unimplemented. The status names below are from that file, not an inference from decomp names. `GetNationalDexEnabled`/`SetNationalDexEnabled` are macro modes of the single `get_set_national_dex_enabled` op; `ScrCmd_29F` becomes `scr_cmd_29f`. “Implemented” is command coverage, not proof that a complete route, animation or save lifecycle works. An explicit stub must continue logging once and counting coverage until implemented; never let a default false answer masquerade as a legitimate event gate.

## 6. Legendary branches and ROM event gates

“Trio” can refer to three different groups in this game. All three are included below. Ordinary availability, distribution entitlement and a Pokémon's fateful-encounter provenance are different state surfaces; owning an item must not bypass the latter two.

**Lake guardians: Azelf, Uxie, Mesprit.** These are already available before CP19: Rowan's post-Distortion lab scene clears their cave hide flags and advances the lab state. Azelf and Uxie use level-50 legendary battles with separate `FLAG_CAUGHT_AZELF`/`FLAG_CAUGHT_UXIE` and hide flags. Mesprit's cave interaction instead activates `ROAMING_SLOT_MESPRIT`; `VAR_ROAMING_MESPRIT_STATE` distinguishes roaming/reset/captured/defeated and the Pokétch branch only checks whether the Marking Map app exists. Hall of Fame restores uncaught stationary guardians and converts defeated Mesprit to reset state. Reuse M5's roaming persistence and HP/status/PID behavior; do not model Mesprit as a stationary battle or require a new National-Dex gate. Core `activate_roaming_pokemon` and legendary battle commands are implemented; no additional trio-specific command stub was found in these scripts. ## 10. Proposed implementation phases and proof

These are planning estimates for an engineer already familiar with the engine, in focused engineer-days, with review and fixture/replay work included but calendar scheduling, external distribution services and large rendering rewrites excluded. They are not benchmark results. Re-estimate after the first real route and first Frontier round. New shared interfaces and save schemas are reviewed contract changes, not one package changing another package's internals. Any importer-output change must bump `IMPORTER_VERSION`.

**M6a — CP20 essentials and League return, 8–13 days.** Prove National mode and both visit orders, Snowpoint's boat, the Fight Area tag battle/blockade, Battle Zone traversal, Mars/Jupiter, Buck's join/leave, the Charon arrest and Buck's Battleground talk, Heatran availability and all five League rematch selections. Implement the required arrest effect and minimal Battleground selection needed for this route; keep optional daily rematches and villa work separately visible. Dependencies: M5 story completion/save, doubles/AI partner, static legendary battle outcomes, field movement and clock.

Proposed `cp20` boundary: start from the CP19 save; obtain the upgrade through the lab scene; sail; win the Fight Area battle; traverse to Stark; defeat Mars/Jupiter; finish the arrest; speak to Buck in the Battleground; return and prove Heatran is available. Require the capture only in a dedicated capture replay, since the plot and rematch unlock do not depend on catching it. Assert `VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE=2`, hidden blockade, `FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN`, `FLAG_TALKED_TO_BATTLEGROUND_BUCK`, correct partner cleanup and real trainer battle logs. Run a second League route and assert rematch IDs. Negative fixture cases cover National completion below threshold, boat before Hall of Fame and Heatran before Buck talk; state round trips prove no scene repeats after reload.

**M6b — Legendary/event branches and optional Battle Zone services, 12–20 days.** Add Temple's party predicate, the distribution/provenance save seam, the three ruin puzzles, orb/warp variants, the lunar/dream events, Shaymin's naming/fateful battle, Hall of Origin's scene, Rotom's complete form/move service, Turnback routing/revisit, weekend Barry, daily Battleground and villa/Ribbon Syndicate services. Suggested split: 3–5 days for event/provenance/party predicates and save migration, 4–7 for event scenes/form services, 2–3 for cave/revisit routing and 3–5 for daily/optional town services. Do not “unlock everything” as a workaround for absent distribution commands.

Use a branch matrix of no item / item only / entitlement only / both, plus caught and uncaught outcomes. Test provenance-sensitive Regigigas independently from Temple's three-species party predicate. Exercise capture, defeat, flee where allowed, blackout, Hall of Fame respawn, multiple concurrent roamers, weekly/daily rollover and frozen-clock reload. Form tests must compare persistent moves/form/held-item effects with battle input after a round trip. The Arceus fixture proves only the encoded gates and scene behavior, never the historical existence of a distribution.

**M6c — complete local Frontier, 25–45 days initially.** Inventory and import facility script/data families, implement the Frontier VM/session seam and save records, then deliver Tower first as the smallest complete challenge loop; follow with Factory, Castle/Arcade and Hall. Budget approximately 5–8 days for the common session/save/script layer, 4–7 for Tower, 4–7 for Factory, 4–7 each for Castle and Arcade, and 4–9 for Hall/ranks and shared integration; parallel work can reduce elapsed time but does not remove the integration risk. Re-estimate the aggregate after Tower's first seven-battle round. Local AI-partner multi belongs to the facility rules; historical network/ranking infrastructure needs a separate product decision and must not be simulated as a functioning remote service.

Every facility needs original fixture records that prove rule validation, deterministic opponent/team generation, its selected AI configuration, round completion, loss/reset, suspend/resume/retire, rental or party restoration, currency changes, print eligibility and save migration. Test boundary streaks immediately before/at/after Brain and round thresholds, separate modes and Hall species/type ranks. Use seeded generation invariants rather than copied reference rosters or exhaustive data tables. A single successful battle is not a facility acceptance test. Browser tests should cover only touched facility/menu/field transitions while working; full ROM browser coverage is the merge gate.

**Verification policy for all phases.** Unit/replay fixtures contain original bytes and original creatures/data; ROM tests read `OPENSINNOH_NDS_ROM` and skip without it. ROM integration uses `romSlice()` and browser integration `seedRomImport(page)` unless testing the importer itself. Split long ROM work across files. A phase is done only after its focused tests, `pnpm check` and ROM `pnpm test` pass with actual results reported, and the full ROM browser suite at merge. This research-only change implements none of these systems and adds no generated content.

Keep unsupported online services visibly separate from complete local gameplay.
