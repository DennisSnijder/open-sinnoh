## Berries

### Soil identity, locations and save

The world has berry-soil object events, not one timer per visible tree model. Their first object-data value is a stable global patch index; graphics ID 100 identifies a patch. Example: Route 208 has patches 26–29 at tiles (435–438,714), script 2800. The manager resolves every plant/water/harvest operation through that index. [res/field/events/events_route_208.json:232–298](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/events/events_route_208.json#L232-L298) [src/berry_patch_manager.c:151–227](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patch_manager.c#L151-L227)

Each stores berry tag number (0 empty; otherwise item minus first berry plus one), stage, minutes left in stage, moisture-minute remainder, replant count, harvested yield, moisture rating, yield rating, mulch and `isGrowing`. ### Planting, stages, watering and yield

Planting consumes one berry through the interaction script, retains soil mulch, and sets planted stage, a full stage timer, zero moisture-minute remainder/replant count/yield, moisture 100 and yield-rating 5. Stage sequence: empty → planted → sprouted → growing → blooming → fruit. Four stage durations reach first fruit. At bloom→fruit, yield is `max(2, baseYield × yieldRating)`. At fruit expiry it returns to **sprouted**, resets rating 5, increments replant count, and eventually clears the entire patch, mulch included. Harvest also clears the entire patch. [src/berry_patches.c:168–184](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patches.c#L168-L184) [src/berry_patches.c:218–251](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patches.c#L218-L251) [src/berry_patches.c:211–215](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patches.c#L211-L215) [res/field/scripts/scripts_berry_tree_interaction.s:268–319](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/field/scripts/scripts_berry_tree_interaction.s#L268-L319)

Each berry's `stageDuration` (hours) comes from its `berryData` in `itemtool/itemdata/nuts_data.narc` (source: `res/items/data/<berry>_berry.json`, e.g. [cheri_berry.json:36](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/items/data/cheri_berry.json#L36-L37)); values range 2–24 h. Import them from the user's ROM; do not hard-code a table. Use `stageDuration×60` minutes, not a species-based timing switch. First fruit takes four durations before mulch; subsequent cycles skip the planted stage.


### Elapsed time and clock changes

The RTC dispatcher compares current and saved calendar seconds: backwards moves the anchor back without applying negative elapsed time; forward: when at least one whole minute elapsed, applies floor(deltaSeconds/60) and sets the anchor to now (discarding the sub-minute remainder); under a minute the anchor stays, so seconds accumulate. It processes all elapsed minutes, including offline gaps, in one call. It decrements the 24-hour clock-change penalty **before** invoking berry and honey updaters. [src/unk_020559DC.c:63–84](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/unk_020559DC.c#L63-L84)

Berry growth skips empty/non-growing patches, drains moisture up to each stage boundary, advances that stage, then consumes the remaining minutes. A large-gap shortcut clears a patch when elapsed time reaches `adjustedStageMinutes × (1 + (3 + fruitRetentionStages) × replantLimit)`; shorter gaps iterate transitions, so they can also exhaust the remaining lifetime of an already-aged patch. Preserve bounded work and the exact threshold. [src/berry_patches.c:288–325](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patches.c#L288-L325)

**Source/decision mismatch to resolve during implementation:** the existing clock decision suggests these systems will consult the clock-change penalty. At this revision neither `BerryPatches_ElapseTime` nor `SpecialEncounter_DecrementHoneyTreeTimers`, nor their dispatcher, gates progress on it. DS load starts the penalty when system/RTC-offset identity differs, resetting the elapsed anchor to current time; a mid-session backwards RTC reading merely rebases. Our browser deliberately detects only a load whose saved clock is ahead (no DS RTC-offset identity). Keep that existing browser policy, and apply subsequent positive berry/honey minutes unconditionally unless a different compatibility rule is chosen explicitly. Do not invent a 24-hour timer freeze. [src/berry_patch_manager.c:97–109](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/berry_patch_manager.c#L97-L109) [src/special_encounter.c:99–120](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/special_encounter.c#L99-L120) [src/system_data.c:102–125](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/system_data.c#L102-L125) [src/game_start.c:119–130](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/game_start.c#L119-L130)

## Poké Radar

### Activation and battery

Use requires no partner, non-cycling avatar and current tile classified as tall grass; do not replace that predicate with any encounter-enabled tile. The bag and registered-item paths share `RefreshRadarChain`. Charge must reach 50; insufficient charge passes the exact missing-step count to the radar script. A charged attempt consumes charge before selecting patches, including when no valid patches result. `RadarChargeStep` increments at the field step hook only while the Radar item is owned, cap 50. Initial charge 0. Ownership and the cap are explicit in [src/pokeradar.c:497–505](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/pokeradar.c#L497-L505). Reuse normal item ownership/story flags; National Dex acquisition belongs to the grant script, not an extra activation predicate absent here. [src/item_use_functions.c:660–674](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/item_use_functions.c#L660-L674) [src/item_use_functions.c:636–657](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/item_use_functions.c#L636-L657) [src/pokeradar.c:417–465](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/pokeradar.c#L417-L465) [include/pokeradar.h:10–15](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/include/pokeradar.h#L10-L15) [src/overlay005/field_control.c:842–854](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay005/field_control.c#L842-L854)

### Patch selection, presentation and continuation

Sample once on each square perimeter around the player, outer-to-inner: Chebyshev distances 4,3,2,1, with 32/24/16/8 candidate tiles. Reject a sampled tile unless it is tall grass, at exactly player height and in the current map header. No retry for rejected candidates, so 0–4 patches can survive. Zero clears the chain. Patch position, ring, continuation flag, normal shake type and shiny flag exist before animation. [src/pokeradar.c:91–144](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/pokeradar.c#L91-L144) [src/pokeradar.c:301–332](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/pokeradar.c#L301-L332)

Continuation probabilities, outer→inner, are 88/68/48/28% after defeating the encounter and 98/78/58/38% after capture. A manually charged reroll passes the **defeat** result regardless of the preceding battle. Continuing patches use the chain's shake type; failed continuation rolls choose soft/hard 50:50, so matching shake type is not proof of continuity. Only continuation patches roll the special shiny flag. Renderer effects distinguish soft, hard and shiny (three effects; shiny is a flag, not a third ordinary encounter-table shake type). ### Encounter override and chain count

Stepping into a surviving patch forces an encounter even if the normal encounter roll failed; roamers are excluded there. A hard patch replaces **species only** in grass slots 4,5,10,11 with the map's four Radar species; existing levels/slot weights remain. A preserved-chain patch reuses the chain's species **and level**, and can force a shiny Pokémon. A non-preserved patch chooses a new slot (including Magnet Pull/Static attraction). First species starts the chain; drawing the same species by chance also increments and retains the original level; a different species clears it. Thus a failed continuation roll is not necessarily a broken chain. [src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c:272–309](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c#L272-L309) [src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c:687–724](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c#L687-L724) [src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c:1148–1160](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c#L1148-L1160) [src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c:1163–1193](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/src/overlay006/wild_encounters.c#L1163-L1193)

## Proposed implementation

Decode `nuts_data.narc` at the boundary; validate item correspondence and payload length. Keep battle item effects on existing `Item` records. ### Clock, field and battle ownership

Use pure minute reducers in core for berry growth and honey countdown; inject them from the game's single elapsed-time dispatcher. Feed the complete positive minute delta once on load/resume/idle update. Preserve per-patch stage/moisture remainders and the clock anchor. Do not call `Date.now()` in core, world, VM or menu code. Frozen replays advance through the existing clock source; ordinary ticks alone do not grow plants or mature honey.

World owns patch visibility activation, dynamic stage graphics/soil moisture, north-facing honey-prop interaction, watering movement/collision/pose and transient Radar patches. Activate preplanted growth at the DS visibility boundary, not whenever a wider desktop camera happens to load a chunk. Resolve changes in off-map saved patches even when no scene is mounted. Reconcile loaded honey animation after time changes so expired honey cannot appear ready.

World encounter selection must check a Radar patch before finalizing ordinary encounter/roamer selection. Preserve the source's RNG order: patch sampling versus post-battle continuation rolls, same-species redraw, ability slot attraction and shiny generation. Use the existing seeded encounter stream; record seeds/state for assertions. Battle outcomes must distinguish defeated/caught/run/player-loss/enemy-fled; charge recovery belongs to one committed-step hook, not render frames or key-repeat ticks.

**Compatibility decision needed:** DS Radar visibility uses `GFXBoxTest` and the original camera volume. Our adjustable field zoom/aspect must not improve continuity or break replay determinism. Recommended rule is a canonical DS camera/visibility region derived from player/camera state, independent of actual viewport; document and test it. Exact projection bounds remain to be measured from source camera setup. Do not let renderer visibility mutate simulation state.

## Remaining uncertainties / follow-up evidence

- Obtain verified US binary locations/member layouts for the initialization and honey-tree map-order tables before importer implementation; the named decomp tables establish semantics but do not by themselves provide ROM offsets. Validate against the supported ROM fingerprint.
- Confirm stage-growth activation against canonical DS visibility, including offscreen patches loaded by neighbouring chunks. Source proves the flag is set for visible patches, not that all engine chunk visibility is equivalent.
- Honey preserved-none re-slather can leave a timer with group 0/table 0; test and retain exact source semantics until a compatibility decision is made. Animation selector meaning/live expiration needs asset-independent inspection of animation metadata.
- The source Munchlax “four trees” comment conflicts with its collision loop. The proposal follows the observed algorithm and marks the discrepancy, rather than silently correcting it.
- Camera bounds for Radar culling need a canonical definition before adjustable viewport integration. Audit exceptional encounter entry points when implementing, including scripted wild/trainer/partner battles; the cited clear paths cover standard ones, not an assertion that every future script entry is handled.
- Clock identity/penalty differs on browser by the existing decision. The source's unconditional berry/honey progression should be agreed explicitly; no new clock policy is adopted by this documentation PR.
