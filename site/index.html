The engine

A new engine, not an emulator

OpenSinnoh doesn't emulate a DS or run the game's code. It's a new engine, written for the browser, that reimplements Platinum's systems. Your ROM is read once, on your device, to import the maps, models, sprites, text and music. Then the engine plays the game itself, in 3D at your screen's resolution.

  • Sharp at any sizeDrawn at your screen's resolution.
  • Made for phonesTouch controls and layout, installable on iPhone and Android.
  • Fast-forwardUp to 8×, hold or toggle.
  • Real savesExport them, move devices.
  • Quality of lifeAuto-run, an item wheel, EV and IV view, a suspend point. All optional.
  • AccessibleScreen readers, remapping, less flashing.

Under the hood

How the engine works

Your ROM is converted once, in your browser, into an open format. From then on the engine reads only that.

  1. Your ROM

    platinum.nds

    The file you made from your own cartridge. It stays on your device.

  3. Importer

    Runs once, in your browser. It converts:

    • Mapsand events
    • Modelsto glTF
    • Texturesto PNG
    • Spritesto sheets
    • Soundsmusic, cries
    • Textevery line
    • Scriptsthe events

    The result is an open pack, kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB). Never uploaded.

  5. Engine

    Loads the pack, any mods on top, and plays the game.

    • Script VMRuns the game's own event scripts.vm
    • WorldMaps, movement, weather, field moves.world
    • BattlesA real battle simulator, with Platinum's data.battle
    • MenusBag, party, PC, Pokédex, marts.menus, services
    • RendererThe 3D world, drawn with three.js.render
    • AudioThe music and sounds, on a DS-style synth.audio

  7. The game

    Pokémon Platinum in your browser, sharp at any size, on desktop, tablet or phone.

    Offline after the first visit.

Written in TypeScript, one package per part (the names above are the folders in packages/). Battles run on the open-source Pokémon Showdown simulator.

Open engine

What an open engine makes possible

The game's data sits in open formats, and every system is code you can read and change. That opens doors the DS kept shut.

  • Mods

    A mod is a small pack that changes the game's records and scripts: swap a starter, rewrite a line, change a route's rules. A demo mod comes with the game, under Options › Data. There's no mod editor yet.

  • Beyond the DS

    Models and textures are standard glTF and PNG, drawn by a modern renderer. The Cinematic style shows what that can do today. New lighting or more detailed art could follow.

  • Quality of life

    Fast-forward, auto-run, an item wheel, EV and IV view, a suspend point, remappable controls and zoom. Things the DS never had, all optional.

  • Fork it

    The code is MIT-licensed. Fork it, add your own ideas, take it somewhere new. Start with the contributing guide.

FAQ

Questions

Is this legal?

OpenSinnoh contains no Nintendo content. You need to own the game and make the ROM from your own cartridge; the rules on backups differ by country. We don't link to ROMs.

Which ROM do I need?

Pokémon Platinum, US version. HeartGold or SoulSilver (US) is an optional add-on.

Is it finished?

Not yet: it's in beta. The story plays to the credits; the post-game is in progress, and it's still being playtested, so expect rough edges and export your saves. Report bugs on Discord.

Where are my saves?

In your browser, on your device. Export a save file from Options › Data to back it up or move it.

Does it send anything?

No account and no ads. We count visits anonymously, without cookies. Error reports only if you allow them.
