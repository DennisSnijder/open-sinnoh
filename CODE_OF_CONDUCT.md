# Code of conduct

OpenSinnoh follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and on the project's Discord.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and take feedback in good faith, and keep personal attacks,
harassment and discriminatory language out of every space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, via Discord (a direct message to a moderator)
or GitHub private reporting. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers apply the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the
behaviour.
